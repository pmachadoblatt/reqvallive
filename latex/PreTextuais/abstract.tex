This dissertation proposes and demonstrates a method and proof of concept
(ReqValLive) for live Success Criteria validation in laboratory multi-agent missions,
using real drone MQTT telemetry at CONCEPTIO, PASS/FAIL reporting, and SysML~v2
skeleton / model write-back (SysON). The research follows Design Science Research.

\vspace{0.8em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Success Criteria. Live validation. MQTT. SysML~v2. UAS. MBSE.
Design Science Research.
